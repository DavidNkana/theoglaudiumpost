**Laudium Biz App – Project Summary**

**1. Core Purpose & Vision**
Laudium Biz App is envisioned as the first truly digital news and community-engagement hub for Laudium—a culturally rich, tightly knit suburb in South Africa that currently relies on print newspapers and WhatsApp groups for local news. By centralizing all regional content in a single, lightweight Progressive Web App (PWA) that will also be packaged as a native Android application, the platform will:

- Streamline discovery and distribution of news
- Empower residents and local businesses to both consume and contribute content
- Provide modern tools (analytics) to inform decisions and drive engagement

**2. Key Stakeholders & Audience**

- **Residents & Community Contributors**: Everyday citizens submitting tips, stories, photos, and events
- **Local Businesses & Advertisers**: Small shops, services, and entrepreneurs seeking targeted, affordable ad placements
- **Editorial Team & Moderators**: Editors who write and publish every article, vet community submissions, and ensure quality

**3. Core Functionality**

1. **Admin-Authored Article Management**

   - Every article is written, illustrated and published by an administrator
   - Rich editor with cover images uploaded to object storage

2. **Admin-Authored Advert Manager**

   - Administrators create, schedule and price every ad in the admin dashboard
   - Tiered “premium visibility” slots for featured placement

3. **Search**

   - Keyword search across articles, categories and tags
   - Archive browsing by date, category and tag

4. **Community News Submission Portal**

   - Simple web form for text, images, and video uploads
   - Workflow for moderation, tagging, and publishing

5. **Push Notifications**

   - Geotargeted alerts (e.g., local events, breaking news)
   - Opt-in preferences by category or urgency

6. **YouTube Integration**

   - Embed local video reporting and community channels seamlessly
   - Analytics on watch-time and engagement

7. **Analytics & Reporting Dashboard**

   - Real-time metrics on readership, ad performance, and submission rates
   - Exportable reports for advertisers and editorial planning

8. **Android + PWA Compatibility**

   - Offline caching and fast loads via service workers
   - Android “installable” wrapper for native-like experience

**4. Unique Differentiators**

- **Community-First Design**: Blends professional journalism with grassroots reporting
- **Human Editorial Ownership**: Every story is written and published by the editorial team
- **Local Monetization Engine**: Affordable, scalable ad model fine­tuned for small businesses
- **Platform Agnosticism**: Seamless transition between browser and Android environments

**5. Monetization Strategy**

The platform is designed to be **financially sustainable and scalable**, with monetization via:

- **Ad Revenue**: Display, video, and “featured listing” packages
- **Ad Placements** (banner, inline, popup, video)
- **Premium Visibility**: Subscription plans for priority placement in feeds and notifications
- **Recurring Placements**: Monthly ad commitments with discounted rates
